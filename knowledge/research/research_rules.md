# Research Rules

## Purpose
- Define the minimum pre-proposal judgment rules for V1.

## Initial Feasibility Judgment
- Rate feasibility only as `high`, `medium`, or `low`.
- Base the rating on simple implementation practicality, demand visibility, and execution clarity.
- Do not produce deep technical or business diligence in V1.

## Initial Return Judgment
- Rate return only as `high`, `medium`, or `low`.
- Base the rating on obvious monetization potential, buyer demand, and likely effort-to-reward balance.
- Do not build complex ROI models in V1.

## Evidence Requirements
- Use only enough evidence to support an initial proposal.
- Mark uncertain items as uncertain instead of guessing.

## Scope Limits
- No long reports.
- No full business plans.
- No deep competitor mapping.
- No investment-grade analysis.

## Reporting Rules
- Proposal output must stay in this structure:
  - project name
  - short summary
  - initial feasibility
  - initial return level
  - reason
