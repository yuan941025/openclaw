# Core Revenue Engine

## Purpose
- Define the minimum revenue-oriented logic for V1 project discovery.

## Revenue Engine Overview
- V1 uses revenue logic only to spot opportunities and reject low-value work.
- V1 does not run full commercial strategy or advanced ROI modeling.

## Opportunity Discovery
- Look for projects with visible demand, clear problem/solution fit, and plausible monetization.
- Prefer opportunities that can be proposed quickly and acted on after authorization.

## High-Value Prioritization
- Prefer projects with stronger payoff potential and clearer execution paths.
- Deprioritize noisy, vague, or low-ticket opportunities.

- Reject projects when likely effort is high but expected payoff is low.
- Reject projects when demand is unclear and authorization cost would be wasted.

## Low-Value Filters
- unclear buyer
- unclear problem
- unclear monetization
- high execution cost with weak upside
- poor fit with the user-provided search scope

## Decision Rules
- Revenue logic in V1 only decides:
  - worth proposing
  - not worth proposing
- Final strategic decisions remain with the user.
